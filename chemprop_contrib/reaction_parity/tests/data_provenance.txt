(100 atom-mapped, fully H-mapped reactions; molecules must be built with keep_h=True).

smiles, ea : copied unchanged from the source file. `ea` is as published.

y_odd, y_even : DERIVED by make_test_data.py, not measured. They are chemically
meaningless and exist only to exercise parity. With logP the Crippen MolLogP of the whole
reactant side and of the whole product side (each side is one Mol, built with
keep_h=True, add_h=False), rounded to 4 decimals:

    y_odd  = logP(pdt) - logP(rct)    exactly odd under reaction reversal
    y_even = logP(pdt) + logP(rct)    exactly even under reaction reversal

`ea` is the free task: activation energy is neither odd nor even.

    python chemprop_contrib/reaction_parity/tests/make_test_data.py <path-to-rxn.csv>
